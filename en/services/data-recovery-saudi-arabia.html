Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I package my drive safely for shipping from Riyadh or Dammam?

Proper packaging is critical. Place the hard drive inside an anti-static bag if available. Wrap the drive in at least three inches of heavy bubble wrap. Place it tightly into a sturdy corrugated cardboard box, ensuring there is absolutely no empty space for the drive to shift or bounce during transit.

How long does the nationwide data recovery process take?
