Laptop & Computer Data Recovery in Jeddah

Your laptop or desktop computer holds your most important documents, from university theses to corporate financial records. When a system crashes, suffers liquid damage, or refuses to boot past the Windows logo, the priority is not fixing the machine—it is rescuing the data inside. Al-Fares Center provides comprehensive laptop and desktop data recovery in Jeddah. Whether you have a traditional spinning hard drive (HDD), a modern Solid State Drive (SSD), or storage chips soldered directly to the motherboard, our engineers can safely extract your files.

Common Causes of Computer Data Loss

Computers can fail for a variety of hardware and software reasons. We routinely handle recoveries involving:

  • Liquid Spills & Motherboard Damage: Coffee or water spilled on a laptop keyboard can instantly short out the motherboard, rendering the computer completely dead.
  • Blue Screen of Death (BSOD) / Boot Loops: The computer continuously restarts or displays a fatal error screen, often caused by operating system corruption or the early stages of storage drive failure.
  • Physical Impact (Dropped Laptops): Dropping a laptop while it is running often causes the internal mechanical hard drive's heads to crash into the magnetic platters.
  • Accidental Deletion or Formatting: A user accidentally formats the wrong partition during a Windows reinstallation or permanently empties the recycle bin containing critical work folders.
  • User Profile Corruption: Windows fails to load the user profile, effectively locking the user out of their Desktop, Documents, and Pictures folders.

Our specialized recovery approach

Recovering data from a failed computer requires isolating the storage media from the damaged host system to prevent further logical corruption or hardware stress:

  1. Safe Storage Extraction: You do not need to disassemble your computer. You bring the entire laptop or desktop tower to our lab, and our technicians carefully extract the internal HDD, SSD, or M.2 NVMe drive using proper anti-static protocols.
  2. Isolated Diagnostic Analysis: We connect the extracted drive directly to our specialized PC-3000 laboratory hardware. This prevents Windows from attempting automatic "chkdsk" or startup repairs, which often permanently destroy failing data structures.
  3. Logical Rebuilding & Decryption: If the computer utilizes BitLocker encryption or has a severely corrupted file system, we utilize forensic software to rebuild the Master File Table (MFT) and decrypt the volume using your recovery key or Windows credentials.
  4. Chip-Off / Micro-Soldering (For Soldered Storage): Some modern thin-and-light laptops have the storage chips (eMMC/UFS) soldered directly to the motherboard. If the motherboard is dead, we perform advanced micro-soldering to read the data directly from the memory chips.
  5. Data Transfer: We extract your User folder (Desktop, Documents, Downloads, etc.) and transfer the files to a secure, healthy external drive for you to use on your new computer.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

I spilled coffee on my laptop. Is the data gone?

Not necessarily. If the liquid shorts the motherboard, the laptop will die, but the internal storage drive is often shielded. Do not try to turn it back on. Bring it to us; we can extract the drive, clean it if liquid penetrated it, and recover your files directly.

The computer shows a Blue Screen of Death (BSOD) and won't boot. Can you help?

Yes. A BSOD often indicates corrupted Windows registry files, failing RAM, or the early stages of a hard drive/SSD failure. We bypass the operating system entirely to safely extract your personal files from the failing drive.

Do I need to remove the hard drive before bringing the laptop?

No, please do not attempt to disassemble your laptop. Many modern laptops have complex chassis designs and delicate ribbon cables. Bring the entire laptop to our center, and our technicians will safely extract the storage device.

My IT department said the motherboard is dead. Can they just put the drive in a new laptop?

If it is an older SATA drive, yes, but Windows may fail to boot due to driver conflicts. If it is a modern laptop with an integrated (soldered) eMMC or NVMe chip, transferring the drive is impossible for a standard IT department. We specialize in recovering data from these soldered chips.

Laptop won't turn on? Don't panic.

Stop trying to power on a damaged or liquid-exposed computer. Contact us to safely retrieve your files.
