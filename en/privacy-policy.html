Data Privacy and Confidentiality Policy

When a storage device fails, the stress of losing critical files is often compounded by the fear of unauthorized access to sensitive information. Whether you are dealing with intimate family photos, confidential financial records, or proprietary corporate data, maintaining absolute data privacy recovery standards is our highest priority at Al-Fares Center. We are unwaveringly committed to ensuring that your private information remains strictly confidential and secure throughout the entire recovery process.

How We Protect Your Data

To provide the most secure file recovery experience, we have implemented strict technical and operational protocols:

  • No Human Browsing of Content: Our engineers do not open, view, or browse your personal files or documents. We utilize the advanced PC-3000 Data Extractor system, which allows us to parse logical records and evaluate file system structures automatically. By analyzing metadata and hex structures rather than file contents, we ensure the integrity of your data without compromising your privacy.
  • Secure Destruction of Temporary Copies: During complex recoveries from malfunctioning drives, it is often necessary to create a sector-by-sector data copy to minimize hardware stress and prevent further degradation. Once your files have been successfully retrieved and delivered to you, we permanently and securely destroy these temporary backup images from our lab systems within a strict 3 to 7-day window.
  • Closed Laboratory Environment: All recovery procedures are performed exclusively within our secure, restricted-access facility. Only authorized engineering personnel are permitted inside the laboratory, ensuring your physical media and extracted data are protected from unauthorized access at all times.

Guarantees for Businesses

Corporate clients require an elevated level of assurance when handling proprietary databases, trade secrets, and client information. We provide specialized confidential data recovery jeddah services tailored specifically for B2B requirements. Our laboratory strictly adheres to professional ethics and industry data protection standards. For organizations requiring formalized legal guarantees, we are fully prepared to review and sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to commencing any diagnostic or recovery work.

Ethical Commitment

Beyond our advanced technical protocols, the foundation of Al-Fares Center is built on uncompromising personal and professional integrity. We handle every storage device with the utmost dignity and respect for the owner's privacy. Our ethical commitment mandates that no client data is ever exploited, retained, or shared under any circumstances.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What happens to the backup copies of my data after delivery?
After you receive your recovered files and verify their integrity, any temporary data copies or sector images created during the recovery process are securely and permanently destroyed from our servers within 3 to 7 days.

2. Does the engineer view my photos or read my documents during recovery?
No. Our engineers rely on advanced PC-3000 Data Extractor tools to read file system structures and metadata records automatically. This automated parsing allows us to verify data integrity without ever needing to open or view the actual contents of your personal files.

3. Can you provide a written NDA for my company before starting the recovery?
Absolutely. We understand the stringent legal and operational requirements of corporate data protection. We are more than happy to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) to provide your organization with the necessary written assurances regarding data confidentiality.
